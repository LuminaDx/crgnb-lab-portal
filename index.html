CR-GNB
CARBAPENEM-RESISTANT GRAM-NEGATIVES

Susceptibility testing support for carbapenem-resistant Gram-negatives

A controlled program for Australian public hospital laboratories. Access educational material on testing methods and request limited laboratory materials for quality susceptibility testing.

Healthcare professional access

This site is intended for healthcare professionals in Australian public hospital and pathology laboratories. Confirm your status and enter your registration number to continue.

Format check only. The program team does not independently verify the number at this step.

Public hospital laboratories only. Private pathology is not eligible. Materials and shipping are provided under the program; laboratories are not charged.
