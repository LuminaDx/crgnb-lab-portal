Complete two-test pack

What a laboratory needs on the bench

These are the consumables that are not already sitting in a standard Australian micro lab (plates, loops, incubators, MALDI). Order only what you do not already hold. Automated instruments and GeneXpert cartridges are out of scope for this pack.

Test 1 · enzyme ID

Carbapenemase 5-plex lateral flow

Immunochromatographic cassette used on a cultured isolate. Reports KPC, NDM, VIM, IMP and OXA-48-like as separate lines in about 15 minutes. No analyser. Room-temperature storage. Typical pack: 20 tests.

Test 2 · screen

Siderophore cephalosporin AST disk 30 µg

Kirby-Bauer disk for aerobic Gram-negatives on standard Mueller-Hinton agar. Do not iron-deplete the plate. Read zone diameter against current EUCAST or CLSI tables. Everyday first method.

Test 2 · confirm

Siderophore cephalosporin BMD panel

Compact broth microdilution panel with the dried agent in doubling dilutions and tubes of iron-depleted cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton broth. Use when the disk is in a technical grey zone or a MIC in mg/L must be reported.

Quality control

Reference Gram-negative strains

Lyophilised E. coli and P. aeruginosa QC strains with published zone and MIC ranges for the disk and BMD methods. Required for NATA method verification and routine QC.
